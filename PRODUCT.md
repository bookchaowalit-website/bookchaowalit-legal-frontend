# Legal Knowledge Base

## Product truth

Legal Knowledge Base is a plain-language index for exploring legal concepts,
document patterns, and questions to ask next. It is educational information,
not legal advice and not a substitute for a licensed professional.

## First release

- Search a small set of plain-language legal topics.
- Filter by topic family and jurisdiction note.
- Open an entry with its meaning, common document touchpoint, and next question.
- Keep every entry visibly scoped and include the legal-information disclaimer.

## Evidence boundary

Entries are self-authored orientation notes. They do not claim current legal
validity, jurisdictional completeness, or attorney review.
